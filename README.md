# 📊 Sales Performance Data Quality Assessment

**Tools:** Power Query | Tableau

An analytical project focused on evaluating sales performance, identifying **data completeness issues**, and understanding **how missing records** can **affect business reporting and decision-making**.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **five years of sales data (2014–2018)** to evaluate business performance across different dimensions, including sales trends, geographic distribution, and warehouse performance.

During the analysis, an apparent decline in sales during 2018 raised an important question: Was the business actually experiencing a performance decline, or was the reported result influenced by incomplete data?

Using Power Query for data preparation and Tableau for visualization, I investigated the issue by validating data completeness and comparing historical sales patterns.

The project demonstrates the importance of data quality assessment before interpreting business performance metrics or making management decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze sales performance across the 2014–2018 period.
- Track key performance indicators and identify sales trends.
- Compare year-over-year business performance.
- Evaluate sales distribution across geographic regions.
- Analyze sales distribution across warehouses.
- Investigate the apparent decline in 2018 sales.
- Assess how missing records can influence reported business performance.

## 🔍 Key Investigation: The 2018 Sales Decline

One of the most important findings emerged during the investigation of the apparent sales decline in 2018.

Rather than immediately interpreting the decline as poor business performance, I examined the underlying data for completeness.

The investigation revealed:

- The 2018 dataset ended on **November 15**, with **December records missing**.
- Historical sales patterns showed that December had contributed meaningful sales quantities in previous years.
- I compared historical November–December patterns to estimate the potential impact of the missing records.
- The estimated impact indicated that the observed decline was largely influenced by incomplete data rather than necessarily reflecting actual business underperformance.

This investigation highlighted why data completeness and validation are essential before drawing conclusions from business dashboards.

## 💡 Key Learnings

Through this project, I gained practical insights into the importance of data quality and its impact on business performance analysis:

- **Data Completeness Matters:** Incomplete reporting periods can significantly distort business performance metrics.
- **Validate Before Comparing:** Year-over-year comparisons should account for differences in data coverage and reporting periods.
- **Historical Trends Provide Context:** Historical patterns can help assess the potential impact of missing records.
- **Question Unexpected Results:** An apparent decline in business performance should be investigated before being interpreted as an actual business issue.
- **Data Quality Drives Reliable Decisions:** Accurate and complete data is essential for meaningful analysis and informed business decisions.
